Work where money, safety or the rules were on the line.

I design software from the first rough sketch to the finished product. I have worked on smart buildings, business tools, augmented reality and online games, for companies like Cooper Lighting Solutions (part of Signify) and Parlay Games.

Mostly I take something complicated and make it simple enough for one person to run.

Think about the lights in a big modern building. It is not really about light switches any more. Thousands of small sensors keep track of what is going on. Which rooms are busy. Which lights have burned out. How much power is being used.

Someone has to see all of that at a glance and decide what to do.

So the lights are almost beside the point.

The real product is the information.
