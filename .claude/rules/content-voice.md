# Rule: Content & voice

Voice for the Product Designer Portfolio.

Full guidance: [`design-system/content/content.md`](../../design-system/content/content.md)

## Tone

- Clear, calm, evidence-led. Domain-literate without cosplay. Confident, never hype.
- **Thesis line:** "the model brings receipts, you keep the decision." Lead with outcomes, not features.
- **Person:** address the reader as "you"; name work plainly (AEGIS, Light ARchitect, hospital lighting).
- Plain language — gloss jargon on first use (e.g. "lighting system" before "DALI-2" on public pages).

## Typography = voice

| Text | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Prose | DM Sans, sentence case, relaxed line-height |
| System/HUD | JetBrains Mono, UPPERCASE, tracked |
| Eyebrows | Mono 9–11px, accent or muted |

## Microcopy

- Lead with the verb ("Take the watch", "Read the trace", "Read the case study").
- **Claims need backing** — tie outcomes to evidence; label design targets vs observed metrics.
- **Errors / empty states:** say what happened and the next step; never blame the user.
- No Lucide/icon library names in user-facing copy.

## Case-study structure

Eyebrow → outcome headline → lead (2–3 sentences) → evidence (screens, stats with footnotes, live demo).
